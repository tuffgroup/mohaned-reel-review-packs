Six signs that a man suffers from loneliness. The first one is that he feels isolated even in public. So he'd rather just spend more time on his own. The second one is he spends a lot of his time numbing and distracting himself with social media or his unhealthy habits or addictions to actually soothe the loneliness that he feels. Number three, he stops taking care of himself and it leads to a lack of confidence, and results in him isolating even more. Number four, he has this deep yearning for the attention from women and the soothing touch from a woman to regulate the wound of loneliness. Number five, he overworks and doesn't have boundaries with his work because it's his cover-up for how he actually feels about himself when he's not working. Number six, he avoids home because home to him feels boring and isolating, yet it's just reminding him of how he actually felt as a little boy. Loneliness will show up in so many different ways depending on the strategies that you've developed to cope and manage from this disconnection from your soul.
