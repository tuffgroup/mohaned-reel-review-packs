So what is the difference between a marketing agency and a creative production studio? Because I'm always very, very clear to say that I run a creative production studio and not a marketing agency. So the difference is that, like myself, as a creative production studio, we come in, we build out the creative strategy, we build out the creative content system that we're going to use on the back end. We build out the brand kit. We then will help research the content, script the content, shoot the content. We take it back, we edit it, we post it. We can do the analysis on it. Literally everything creative leading up to the point where somebody would start running ads, email marketing campaigns, anything of that nature. That's where I draw the line. And the reason I do this is very simple. It's always been the same answer, and it is because myself and my team, we are fucking killers when it comes to creative. We don't know shit about ads. So, why would I dilute my business by trying to compete with people that are killers at ads? And then on the flip side, I think some marketing agencies should do the same where they're killer at ads, but then they are diluting their business because they're trying to get into the creative space when they're just kind of half-assing it, because that's not their specialty. They're just trying to make do. And then the solution to this is actually what I do with a lot of marketing agencies, and we have strategic partnerships where I take care of the creative side, they take care of the ads side. It's almost like selling it like a bundle, two companies. So yeah, I guess moral of the story is don't dilute your business.
