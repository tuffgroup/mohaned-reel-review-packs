Kevin Gates was born Kevin Jerome Gilyard (February 5, 1986) in New Orleans, Louisiana, USA, and was raised in Baton Rouge. He is an American rapper, singer, songwriter, and entrepreneur known for his raw storytelling, emotional honesty, and distinctive Southern sound. Growing up in Baton Rouge, Gates experienced a difficult upbringing and began using music as a way to document what he saw and lived through. His early mixtapes helped him develop a loyal following before he broke into the mainstream. His breakthrough came through projects such as The Luca Brasi Story, Stranger Than Fiction, and By Any Means. In 2016, he released his debut studio album Islah, which reached No. 2 on the Billboard 200 and featured major hits such as “2 Phones” and “Really Really.” Gates is known for turning his personal experiences into music. His lyrics often explore pain, relationships, ambition, struggle, self-reflection, and personal growth, which has helped him connect deeply with his audience. He continued building his career with projects including I’m Him, Khaza, The Ceremony, and I’m Him 2. His ability to remain authentic while constantly evolving has made him one of the most distinctive voices in modern Southern hip-hop. Kevin Gates’ story is ultimately about transformation—using difficult experiences as fuel, staying true to yourself, and continuing to grow.
